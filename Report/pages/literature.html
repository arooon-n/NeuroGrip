Literature Review

The literature review focuses on the advancements in Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) technology, particularly in the context of controlling robotic systems using EEG signals. The review highlights key studies and methodologies that have contributed to the development of effective BCI systems, emphasizing the integration of deep learning techniques for signal classification and robotic control.

BCI & EEG Overview

BCIs enable brain-to-device control. EEG is the most used non-invasive method.

Traditional methods and advancements in Deep Learning

Traditional methods (CSP, SVM) had low accuracy & poor real-time use. Deep learning (CNN-BiLSTM) improves multi-directional movement decoding. Jeong et al. (2020): Achieved 60% execution & 43% imagery success in 3D control.

BCI Competition IV Insights

BCI Competition IV showed CSP-based methods still strong for classification.

Key Challenges

Low SNR, user variability, real-time performance.

Future Scope

Adaptive learning, hybrid BCIs, improved usability.

The review also discusses the challenges faced in real-time BCI applications, including signal noise, user variability, and the need for personalized training protocols. By synthesizing findings from various research efforts, this literature review provides a comprehensive overview of the current state of BCI technology and its potential for enhancing human-robot interaction.
